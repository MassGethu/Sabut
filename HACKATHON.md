## Submission content

- **Title** — Sabūt · Satellite evidence for rice incentive inspections
- **Tagline** — See which rice claims need a field visit.
- **Description** — Sabūt helps officers prioritize field visits for direct-seeded rice incentive claims. It turns Sentinel-1 radar histories into traceable plot evidence, flags where observations are insufficient, and explains which claim would benefit most from a visit. The officer records the decision, and the farmer receives a clear status and a way to request a review.
- **Planned stack** — Python, Sentinel-1, Ricemapper features, LightGBM, FastAPI, SQLite, React, and GeoJSON. List the data provider and libraries actually used in the submitted build.
- **GitHub** — Use the team's repository URL. Verify a judge can open it and follow the README.
- **Live demo URL** — Include it if available. Keep the cached local demo runnable for the team's walkthrough.

In the README or walkthrough, demonstrate the neighboring-plot comparison, the one-visit budget, Meena's logged actions, the farmer review request, the real extraction, and the benchmark and replay results. Credit Ricemapper explicitly and identify which data and outcomes are simulated.
